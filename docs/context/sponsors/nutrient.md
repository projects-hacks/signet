## What they are selling

A deterministic document platform for agents and humans. REST APIs and SDKs to
parse, extract, convert, redact, digitally sign and archive, plus an embeddable
DWS Viewer for human review, all with replayable output and audit trails they
argue an AI-alone approach cannot guarantee.

Their framing: the best document work is not one magic call, it is a pipeline.
Pull the data out, judge how confident you are, bring a human in exactly where
it matters, and keep a record of every step.

## The one rule

The project must use DWS, API or SDK or Viewer, for at least one core document
operation, meaningfully. Not a single throwaway call.

## Bonus respect

Pipelines that lean on what makes DWS different: deterministic, auditable
output, with a human in the loop where a guess is not acceptable.

## Their sparks, ideas rather than requirements

Each is anchored to a regulation landing now: customer onboarding regulators
will accept, PDF invoice to compliant e-invoice (France mandate 1 September,
mid event), cross checking a bundle of trade documents for a customs penalty
mismatch, the US mortgage appraisal format cutover at the end of 2026, and
redact then release under EU AI transparency rules live from August 2026.
